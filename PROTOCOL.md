# Study 29: Check a solar-resource shortlist before treating it as a project shortlist

## Decision, scope and acceptance

How stable is a two-location solar-resource shortlist across years and missing-day scenarios? Deliver a source-backed implementation, complete exclusions and candidate outcomes, original figures, a practical first layer and an independent manuscript. This is a resource-screening exercise at gridded locations, not a plant design, land assessment, physical investment recommendation or climate-trend attribution.

Success requires verified source rights/attribution and units; exact captured daily responses; independent two-format/native-cell and calendar checks; predefined information boundaries and six candidates; development-only choice followed by a committed dependency freeze before temporal/geographic outcome summaries; all negative findings, rankings and ties; independent arithmetic, finite boundary fixtures, original-preserving offline replay, inspected figures/PDF and canonical publication audit; exact candidate/main Linux checks, tested/merged tree identity and a remote annotated tag. Record available shared account usage and honest workload proxies.

## Source decision and prior exposure

The historical proposal names NSRDB. Its official developer documentation requires an API key and an active email for the convenient per-site export; no account, email subscription or new credential is created here. Its current instructions were inspected but no radiation outcome file was retrieved. Use NASA Langley POWER's documented public daily point API as a different official solar-resource source. NASA POWER is not NSRDB; neither is silently substituted in the catalog.

Before collection, inspected official POWER API, source, unit/time, missing-solar-data, referencing and NASA science-data policy pages. The source documentation says historical solar flux from 2001 onward uses CERES SYN1deg; near-real-time FLASHFlux and pre-2001 SRB have different characteristics. Restrict to 2001–2023 to avoid the stated 2001 source transition and current low-latency stream. Do not claim no other source revisions or an independently verified homogeneous climate record. Documentation's Ed4.1 validation tables and Ed4.2 source description are not assumed to establish this local cohort's uncertainty. No ground station records or radiation rankings have been fetched/scored before this protocol.

NASA's scientific-data license guidance distinguishes marked licenses, NASA-led mission data and other as-is data. Retain POWER/NASA Langley/Earth Science Division attribution, service/version/access date, source links and change notice. Publish a minimal factual daily projection and computed results only, with the actual returned source/version metadata. No blanket right to NASA logos, source page text or unrelated third-party products. No restricted notice may be ignored. The source's requested notification of publications/redistribution is noted; this task does not authorize unsolicited external email.

## Fixed geographic requests and calendar

Twelve WGS84 points are a deliberately chosen bounded example, not a probability sample or existing development opportunity. Western cohort: the Cartesian product latitude 32.5, 34.5, 36.5 and longitude −114.5,−112.5. Eastern cohort: the same latitudes with longitude −108.5,−106.5. IDs W01–W06 and E01–E06 follow ascending latitude then longitude. Treat them as requested grid locations, not named stations or physical parcels. The official primary solar source is 1 degree resolution; distinct requested coordinates do not establish independent weather or spatial errors. Verify returned coordinate/grid bindings and retain any duplicated source series; do not multiply identical cells into independent observations.

Capture ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DWN only, community SB, 20010101 through 20231231, time-standard UTC, as JSON and CSV for each point. The API is documented as supporting these formats. Sequentially request each exact URL once with a short gap; stop on rate limits or access denial, no identity changes, proxy rotation or insecure TLS. Preserve every response/error byte privately before parsing, with UTC start/end, status, relevant headers, URL, byte count, SHA256, protocol commit and runtime. Do not overwrite prior captures. Reconcile the independently decoded CSV and JSON calendars, values, fill states, coordinates, parameter units and declared source metadata. Keep unavailable days in a full leap-aware daily ledger. NASA's API documentation warns against repeated requests for the same cell; the two-format comparison follows its explicit multiple-format guidance and remains bounded at 24 requests.

Require the community unit to establish daily irradiation in kWh/m2/day; equivalent explicit spellings are accepted only when dimensionally identical, not W/m2without a documented integration conversion. Daily values must be finite and nonnegative or a declared source fill value; unknown invalid states remain unavailable. No ad hoc outlier trimming or positive-value selection. Require exact calendar keys and no conflicting duplicate point/date cells. Gate a point-year at ≥99 percent valid days and ≥95 percent in every calendar month. The primary annual resource is the mean over available valid daily irradiation, not an imputed annual total; disclose remaining seasonal missingness. Missing days do not become zero. Failed point-years remain in coverage ledgers.

Before ranking, require at least four distinct source cells per cohort with valid point-years for all 2001–2023 years. If the gate fails, preserve and stop rather than lowering it after scores. Retain all 12 planned points and reasons even when excluded. This gate establishes a finite descriptive comparison, not power or representativeness.

## Forecast boundary, candidates and selection

At the start of target year Y, each rule sees only qualified annual daily-mean resource from years <Y at that point. These are current retrospectively retrieved historical estimates, not verified original publication vintages; do not claim exact-day historical access or real-time operational performance. For later held years, earlier held annual outcomes become input history as they would in a rolling-origin simulation, but no parameter is retuned. Export source year memberships for every prediction.

Six prespecified rules: persistence(last year),trailing mean 3, trailing mean 5, trailing mean 10, trailing median 10, and trailing lower quantile 20 percent over 10 years. The quantile is the nearest-rank order statistic at ceil(0.2*n),without interpolation. Windows and quantile are declared simple diagnostics, not optimized or source-prescribed choices. All targets have ten prior years available before candidate comparison. No feature scaling, neural network or unnecessary learned model. Exact rational arithmetic operates on retained decimal resource tokens; plotted/displayed precision is not physical source accuracy.

Within each cohort and target year, rank descending by predicted resource and select two points. Ties choose ascending point ID; export the entire tied score set, full candidate ranking and tie status at the shortlist boundary. Score regret as the actual target-year mean resource of its best two points minus that of the rule's selected two. Units:kWh/m2/day. The oracle uses target outcomes for scoring only, never prediction. Also report all-point MAE, Spearman correlation using midranks(with undefined constant vectors retained as unknown),top-two overlap and every annual block. No selected-location multiplication into total installed energy or currency.

Use western target years 2011–2016 for development. Select the smallest mean annual shortlist regret, then all-point MAE, then the fixed candidate order above. Export all six results and the full selection calculation. Commit the protocol, source projection, eligibility, prediction/checking code, fixtures and development outputs with hashes before held summaries. Held targets are 2017–2023 in both western and eastern cohorts. Eastern earlier resource history is allowed solely as local input covariates; no eastern ranking outcome is used for candidate choice. State this as geographic transfer with local history, not absence of all eastern data. East 2011–2016 decision summaries are not executed. At least four common qualified cells per cohort is required; no within-year changing shortlist population.

## Missing-day sensitivity and interpretation

After freeze, apply a fixed stress analysis to complete observed target-year daily series only. Remove one contiguous block of 1, 7 or 30 days starting on the first day of each calendar month, without wrapping past year-end. Estimate annual daily mean from remaining observations. Compare with the intact mean and intact within-cohort top-two shortlist; export every point/year/start/duration, available-day denominator, absolute/signed bias, boundary ties and shortlist change. This is a controlled artificial removal, not the actual satellite missingness mechanism or a calibrated probability of future data loss. Source-incomplete years remain separately unavailable for intact-reference stress analysis. Do not choose removals after seeing which favors a rule, fill missing data or turn the perturbation into new held tuning.

Resource estimates do not equal photovoltaic electricity. No plane-of-array conversion, tilt, shading, inverter, temperature, soiling, storage or finance model is fitted. If a purely illustrative conversion is shown, it must use an explicit fixed horizontal area and efficiency, be labeled hypothetical and leave proportional ranking unchanged; it cannot be reported as actual output or savings. Prefer reporting radiation resource directly. No station-held-out accuracy claim without actual independently measured stations. The relevant decision is whether a small resource ranking difference survives the stated cases and which site-specific measurements a reviewer still needs.

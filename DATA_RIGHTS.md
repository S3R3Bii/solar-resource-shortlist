# Source scope and attribution

NASA Langley Research Center POWER is the proposed source. The minimal factual daily projection will retain source/service/version/access date and NASA Earth Science Division attribution, with exact request and hash evidence. See https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/referencing/ and https://science.data.nasa.gov/about/license . Any returned restrictive notice must be honored; no blanket rights to logos, source text or unrelated third-party products are claimed.

The original NSRDB proposal remains historical; no NSRDB outcome file has been captured. The implementation uses POWER only if source metadata, units, rights and the protocol's coverage gates pass. Originals, source pages, unneeded contact metadata, secrets and formal-publication drafts stay private. Derived ratios, rankings and missing-day scenarios are new calculations, not NASA validation or endorsement. Publication/redistribution notification requests are noted without sending external email.
